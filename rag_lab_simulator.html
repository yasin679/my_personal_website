What to remember

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Before the model answers, a retrieval step finds relevant material from your knowledge source.
This simulator uses a lightweight, transparent word-feature scoring method—not a real neural embedding model or an LLM. It illustrates the workflow, not production-quality semantic search.

Glossary

Corpus — the documents you search.

Chunk — a manageable piece of a document.

Embedding — a numeric representation of meaning.

Similarity — a score used to rank candidate chunks.

Grounding — basing an answer on retrieved evidence.

Learning note: a production RAG system usually adds a real embedding model, vector index, metadata filters, prompt assembly, an LLM, citations, evaluation, and access controls.
